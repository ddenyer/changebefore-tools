Our diagnostic approach

PACE²™

Whether change is required at the organisation, business unit or team level, getting the diagnosis right before committing to an intervention is not a preliminary step. It is the most important work.

PACE²™ is ChangeBefore’s proprietary diagnostic framework, grounded in evidence-based management, CIMO-logic, and thirty years of published organisational research. It provides the structure for moving from what you are seeing, to what is causing it, to what to do about it — and for testing whether that response is actually working.

The framework operates on two levels. PACEi is the diagnostic process — four phases that guide how we work with you. PACEii is what that process produces — four conditions that reliably precede effective performance. Both matter. Click any segment to explore.
