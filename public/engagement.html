The Engagement Profile

A short self-assessment for leaders. About 4 minutes.

You'll respond to 19 statements about your team. For each one, rate it the way your team would — if they were being completely honest and didn't have to face you afterwards.

Each item has two opposite statements. Slide the marker toward the one your team would lean towards, and how strongly. Sit it in the middle if neither pole would feel right to them.
